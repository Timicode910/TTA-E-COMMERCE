OUR STORY

Furniture that works as hard as you do.

We make desks and chairs for the hours you actually spend at them.

WHERE WE STARTED

A better workday begins with a better setup.

Branch began with a simple frustration: most office furniture is built to be bought, not to be sat in all day. Chairs that ached by lunch. Desks that wobbled. Setups that never quite fit the person using them.

So we started making the pieces we wanted ourselves. Furniture that adjusts to you, looks calm in a room, and holds up for years. Every product goes through the same question: will this make someone’s workday a little easier?

Today we design and ship from Canada, and we still test every chair and desk by working at it.
